
# My Favorite Series 😄

## 1. One Punch Man

<img width="510" height="580" alt="One Punch Man" src="https://d32qys9a6wm9no.cloudfront.net/images/tvs/poster/73/3f44f067a0d55642ccb19cd3283e0b51_original.jpg" />

*One Punch Man follows Saitama, an ordinary man who becomes an incredibly powerful superhero after three years of intense training. He becomes so strong that he can defeat almost any enemy with just a single punch. However, because of his overwhelming strength, Saitama becomes bored with fighting and struggles to find a worthy opponent. Alongside his disciple Genos, he joins the Hero Association to protect humanity from dangerous monsters and villains. The series is known for its incredible action scenes, hilarious comedy, powerful characters, and unique superhero story.*

**One Punch Man Season Guide**

**The One Punch Man anime has three released seasons, with a continuation of Season 3 scheduled for 2027. Here's a brief guide to the seasons and their arcs:**

* **Season 1** Introduction Arc, House of Evolution Arc, Hero Association Arc, and Alien Conquerors Arc - Introduces Saitama and Genos as they fight dangerous monsters, join the Hero Association, and face the powerful alien Boros.

* **Season 2** Human Monster Saga - Introduces Garou, a powerful martial artist who hunts heroes while Saitama continues his adventures as a professional hero.

* **Season 3** Monster Association Arc - Follows the conflict between the Hero Association and Monster Association as powerful heroes face dangerous monsters and Garou continues growing stronger.

* **Season 3 Part 2** Monster Association Arc Continuation - Scheduled for January 2027 and continues the battle against the Monster Association.

## 2. Jujutsu Kaisen

<img width="510" height="580" alt="Jujutsu Kaisen" src="https://d2ueuvlup6lbue.cloudfront.net/variants/production/store/fitpad/520/728/8hen0n38pr06t6kukt1rk7ow5p51/_.jpg" />

*Jujutsu Kaisen follows Yuji Itadori, a high school student who accidentally becomes the vessel of Ryomen Sukuna, the King of Curses, after swallowing one of his cursed fingers. Yuji enters Tokyo Jujutsu High School, where he learns to control cursed energy and fight dangerous curses alongside Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki. Under the guidance of the powerful sorcerer Satoru Gojo, Yuji faces deadly enemies and discovers the dark secrets of the jujutsu world. The anime is known for its intense battles, supernatural powers, emotional moments, and unforgettable characters.*

**Jujutsu Kaisen Season Guide**

**The Jujutsu Kaisen anime has three released seasons and a fourth season announced. Here's a brief guide to the seasons and their arcs:**

* **Season 1** Introduction Arc, Cursed Womb Arc, Vs. Mahito Arc, Kyoto Goodwill Event Arc, and Death Painting Arc - Introduces Yuji Itadori and his classmates as they train to become jujutsu sorcerers and fight dangerous curses.

* **Movie** Jujutsu Kaisen 0 - Follows Yuta Okkotsu, a young sorcerer who learns to control his powerful cursed spirit under the guidance of Satoru Gojo.

* **Season 2** Hidden Inventory Arc and Shibuya Incident Arc - Explores the past of Satoru Gojo and Suguru Geto before focusing on the devastating battle between sorcerers and curses in Shibuya.

* **Season 3** The Culling Game Part 1 - Follows Yuji and his allies as they enter a deadly competition organized by Kenjaku.

* **Season 4** The Culling Game Part 2 - An announced continuation of the dangerous Culling Game, featuring powerful sorcerers and intense battles.

## 3. Dragon Ball Z

<img width="450" height="auto" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9d04c518-2372-41ac-91fe-94e2b7bed2b2" />


*Dragon Ball Z follows Son Goku, a powerful Saiyan warrior who protects Earth from dangerous enemies threatening humanity and the universe. Alongside his friends Vegeta, Gohan, Piccolo, and Krillin, Goku faces powerful villains such as Frieza, Cell, and Majin Buu. Throughout the series, the characters undergo intense training, unlock incredible transformations, and participate in legendary battles. Dragon Ball Z is known for its iconic Super Saiyan transformations, powerful energy attacks, unforgettable rivalries, and inspiring lessons about determination, friendship, and surpassing one's limits.*

**Dragon Ball Z Saga Guide**

**The Dragon Ball Z anime consists of 291 episodes covering four major story arcs and several smaller sagas. Here's a brief guide to the major sagas:**

* **Saga 1** Saiyan Saga - Introduces Goku's Saiyan origins as Raditz, Nappa, and Vegeta arrive on Earth, leading to intense battles against powerful Saiyan warriors.

* **Saga 2** Namek Saga - Gohan, Krillin, and Bulma travel to Planet Namek to search for the Dragon Balls while encountering dangerous enemies.

* **Saga 3** Frieza Saga - Goku and his allies battle the powerful Emperor Frieza, leading to Goku's legendary transformation into a Super Saiyan.

* **Saga 4** Garlic Jr. Saga - Gohan, Piccolo, and Krillin defend Earth against Garlic Jr. while Goku is away.

* **Saga 5** Android Saga - Future Trunks arrives to warn the Z Fighters about dangerous androids that will threaten humanity.

* **Saga 6** Imperfect Cell Saga - Introduces Cell, a powerful artificial life-form who absorbs humans and androids to achieve his perfect form.

* **Saga 7** Cell Games Saga - Goku, Gohan, and the other Z Fighters participate in the Cell Games to save Earth from destruction.

* **Saga 8** Great Saiyaman Saga - Follows Gohan as he attends high school and secretly protects the city as the superhero Great Saiyaman.

* **Saga 9** World Tournament Saga - Goku and his friends reunite to participate in the World Martial Arts Tournament.

* **Saga 10** Majin Buu Saga - Introduces the powerful Majin Buu and the evil wizard Babidi, leading to dangerous battles and Vegeta's emotional sacrifice.

* **Saga 11** Fusion Saga - Goten and Trunks learn the Fusion Dance while Goku and Vegeta explore powerful new transformations to defeat Majin Buu.

* **Saga 12** Kid Buu Saga - Goku and Vegeta face the final and most dangerous form of Majin Buu in an epic battle to save the universe.
